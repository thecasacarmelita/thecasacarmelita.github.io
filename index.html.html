Welcome to

The Casa Carmelita Family Resort

A private family resort in Purok 7, Banaoang, Mangaldan, Pangasinan, created as a welcoming place where families and friends can relax, reconnect and celebrate meaningful moments together.

The Casa Carmelita Family Resort was inspired by Carmelita, whose warmth, love of family and spirit of togetherness are at the heart of the resort. Our aim is simple: to provide a clean, comfortable and peaceful setting where every gathering can feel personal, relaxed and memorable.

Guests can enjoy refreshing swimming pools, landscaped outdoor spaces, comfortable living and dining areas, entertainment facilities and practical amenities for an easy, enjoyable stay. Whether you are planning a quick swim, a family day out, an overnight escape or an important celebration, The Casa Carmelita Family Resort offers the privacy and flexibility to enjoy the occasion at your own pace.

Choose your experience

Stay. Swim. Celebrate.

From relaxed family outings to overnight stays and milestone celebrations, The Casa Carmelita Family Resort offers flexible options designed for quality time, comfort and privacy.

☀️ Day Tour

Enjoy a refreshing daytime escape with exclusive resort access—ideal for family bonding, casual celebrations, swimming and relaxed get-togethers.

Perfect for: birthdays, family outings, barkada gatherings and simple celebrations.

🌙 Night Tour

Experience The Casa Carmelita Family Resort in the evening, when the pool lights and peaceful surroundings create a relaxed atmosphere for dining, swimming, karaoke and celebrations.

Perfect for: after-work gatherings, birthdays, reunions and evening celebrations.

🏡 Overnight Stay

Stay longer and enjoy a more unhurried experience with access to sleeping accommodation and overnight amenities according to your selected package.

Perfect for: family staycations, visiting relatives, small groups and guests who want more time together.

🎉 Events & Celebrations

Make special occasions more memorable in a private setting with pool, garden and gathering spaces that can suit birthdays, reunions and other family events.

Perfect for: milestone birthdays, family reunions, intimate celebrations and group occasions.

💚 Private Resort Experience

Enjoy the freedom of a private venue without the feel of a crowded public resort. Spend your time swimming, eating, relaxing and celebrating with the people who matter to you.

Best for: guests who value privacy, comfort and quality time.

📩 Custom Group Enquiry
